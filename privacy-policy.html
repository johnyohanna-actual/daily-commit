Daily Commit Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version: your habits, to-dos and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone. The only information that leaves your phone is what you choose to share on a team leaderboard: a display name, your streaks and how many of today's habits you've finished. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Daily Commit ("the app") is a habit and streak tracker made by John Yohanna, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy can be sent to yohannaappdevelopment@gmail.com.

Information stored only on your device

The following is saved on your iPhone and is not sent to us or anyone else:

  • The habits you create, their schedules and goals.
  • Your daily results, streak history and timer progress.
  • Your to-do list and completed to-do history.
  • Your settings, such as the time your day starts and reminder times.

The home screen widget reads a small summary of this information from storage on your iPhone. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and are not sent through any server.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads your step count, exercise minutes and workouts so that fitness habits can check themselves off. The app never writes to Apple Health.

  • Health data is used only to show your habit progress inside the app.
  • Health data stays on your iPhone. It is never sent to us, never included in leaderboards, and never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • You can turn off access at any time in the Health app under your profile, then Apps, then Daily Commit.

Team leaderboards (optional)

Leaderboards are used only if you create or join a team. When you do, the app saves the following in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) service so your teammates can see it:

  • The display name you enter (it does not have to be your real name).
  • Your current streak and best streak.
  • How many of today's habits you've finished and how many were due, plus the date.
  • The team's name and invite code.

Your habit names, to-dos and Health data are never shared. Leaderboard entries are linked to an anonymous identifier that Apple creates for this app, not to your Apple ID email address. Anyone with your team's invite code can see the team's leaderboard, so share codes only with people you want on your team.

Leaving a team deletes your entry from that team's leaderboard. Leave your teams before deleting the app; if you delete the app first and want your entries removed, contact us and we will remove them.

Leaderboard data is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Using leaderboards requires being signed in to iCloud on your device.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-up with us.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use this only to fix problems and improve the app.

Deleting your data

  • On your iPhone: deleting the app removes all habits, to-dos and history stored by it. You can also clear finished to-dos in Settings, then To-Do History.
  • Leaderboards: leave a team in the Teams tab to remove your entry, or contact us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
